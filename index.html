04 / Work method

A repeatable way to troubleshoot.

Structured problem-solving reduces guesswork and makes support easier to hand over, review, and improve.

STEP 01

Understand

Clarify the symptom, scope, impact, and what changed before the issue began.

STEP 02

Investigate

Check the basics and collect evidence using appropriate system and network tools.

STEP 03

Test a fix

Make one controlled change at a time, then verify the original problem is resolved.

STEP 04

Document

Record findings, the fix, verification, and any follow-up needed.
